Community Guidelines

How we build a great community together

These guidelines reflect the culture we're building at Armteqi – a place where everyone feels welcome, curious, and supported.

Version 1.0 Effective: July 23, 2026 For all members

Welcome to Armteqi. We're a student-led technology community built on curiosity, respect, collaboration, and ethical learning. Whether you're just getting started or already have experience, you are welcome here. These guidelines exist to help us build a community where everyone can learn, grow, and contribute meaningfully.

Respect

Treat everyone with dignity and kindness.

Learn

Stay curious and ask questions.

Share

Help others grow and succeed.

Protect

Use technology responsibly and ethically.

01 Why These Guidelines Matter

Armteqi is more than just a place to learn technology. It's a community where people support each other, share knowledge, and grow together.

These guidelines aren't a list of restrictions. They're a shared understanding of how we treat each other, how we learn, and how we build something meaningful together. When we all follow these guidelines, Armteqi becomes a place where everyone thrives.

Our goal is to create a community where everyone feels comfortable asking questions, sharing knowledge, and making mistakes – because that's how we learn.

02 Be Respectful

Respect is the foundation of our community. Every member deserves to be treated with dignity and kindness, regardless of their background, experience level, or opinions.

Treat others with dignity and respect

Everyone has different preferences and boundaries. Listen and adjust.

Communicate with kindness

Words matter. Choose language that builds up, not tears down.

Embrace diversity

We're a global community. Different perspectives make us stronger.

Listen actively

Seek to understand before responding. Everyone's voice matters.

03 Be Curious

Curiosity is what drives learning. At Armteqi, we encourage everyone to explore, ask questions, and never stop learning.

  • Embrace the unknown – it's okay not to know something. That's where learning begins.
  • Ask "why" – understanding the reasoning behind things is more valuable than memorizing facts.
  • Share interesting discoveries – if you find something cool, share it with the community.
  • Challenge yourself – step outside your comfort zone. That's where growth happens.

04 Help Others Learn

One of the most powerful ways to learn is by helping others. When you share your knowledge, you reinforce your own understanding and contribute to the community's growth.

  • Welcome beginners – every expert was once a beginner. Be patient and encouraging.
  • Share resources – recommend articles, courses, tools, or projects that helped you.
  • Explain concepts clearly – break down complex ideas into understandable pieces.
  • Recognize progress – celebrate learning milestones, no matter how small.

Celebrate progress, not perfection. Everyone is at a different stage in their journey. We value growth over expertise.

05 Keep Conversations Constructive

To make our community valuable for everyone, we try to keep discussions focused on learning, technology, and topics related to Armteqi's mission.

  • Stay on topic – use appropriate channels and threads for specific discussions.
  • Share thoughtfully – ensure your contributions add value to the conversation.
  • Respect others' time – be concise and clear in your communication.
  • Use the right platform – some conversations are better in group chats, others in forums.

06 Practice Ethical Technology

Armteqi teaches cybersecurity and technology skills. With that knowledge comes responsibility. We use our skills to protect, not to harm.

  • Use knowledge for good – apply your skills defensively and ethically.
  • Get permission first – never test security on systems you don't own or have explicit permission to test.
  • Report vulnerabilities responsibly – if you find a security issue, report it privately.
  • Share responsibly – when discussing exploits or vulnerabilities, focus on education, not exploitation.

Armteqi does not support or condone any illegal or unethical activity. We're here to build the next generation of ethical cybersecurity professionals.

07 Give Credit Where It's Due

Armteqi is built on collaboration and open knowledge. When you use or share someone else's work, proper attribution is a sign of respect and integrity.

  • Credit creators – when sharing code, articles, or ideas, link to the original source.
  • Respect licenses – understand and follow the licensing terms of the resources you use.
  • Don't plagiarize – if you didn't create it, don't claim you did.
  • Share your own work – if you've built something useful, share it with the community.

08 Respect Privacy

Privacy is a core value at Armteqi. We respect each other's boundaries and handle personal information with care.

  • Don't share others' info – never post someone's personal details without their explicit consent.
  • Keep private conversations private – if someone shares something in confidence, respect that.
  • Use community platforms responsibly – understand the privacy implications of the tools we use.
  • Be mindful of screen sharing – before sharing your screen, close sensitive tabs or documents.

For more details on how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy.

09 Meaningful Participation

Armteqi is a community for learning and collaboration, not a marketplace. We value contributions that enrich the community and help others grow.

  • Share relevant content – if you share a resource, make sure it adds value to the community.
  • No unsolicited DMs – don't message members with promotional content without their consent.
  • Promote thoughtfully – if you have a project, share it in appropriate channels and be open to feedback.
  • Respect the community – don't use Armteqi primarily for personal promotion.

10 Ask Questions Without Fear

No question is "too basic." Everyone starts somewhere, and asking questions is how we learn. We encourage curiosity and support each other's learning journey.

  • Ask freely – if you don't understand something, there's probably someone else who doesn't either.
  • Answer kindly – when responding to questions, be patient and encouraging.
  • Learn out loud – share what you're learning, even if you're not an expert yet.
  • Admit when you don't know – it's more honest than giving incorrect information.

Mistakes are part of learning. We embrace them as opportunities to grow, not as failures to be ashamed of.

11 Accept Feedback Gracefully

Feedback is a gift. It helps us grow, improve, and see things from different perspectives. At Armteqi, we give and receive feedback with respect and openness.

  • Receive with an open mind – feedback is about your work, not your worth.
  • Give constructively – focus on specific behaviors or work, not on the person.
  • Be specific – offer actionable suggestions when giving feedback.
  • Say thank you – acknowledge the time and thought someone put into helping you improve.

12 Report Problems Responsibly

If you see something that doesn't align with these guidelines or our Code of Conduct, please speak up. Reporting issues is how we keep Armteqi safe and welcoming.

  • Report promptly – the sooner we know, the sooner we can address it.
  • Be specific – include details about what happened and when.
  • Trust the process – the Trust & Safety Team will handle reports fairly and confidentially.
  • No retaliation – Armteqi does not tolerate retaliation against those who report concerns.

We review every report fairly and without retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. To report a concern, email us at armteqi.community@gmail.com or use the Trust Center.

13 Enforcement

While we believe in education and second chances, we also take the health of our community seriously. If someone repeatedly or seriously violates these guidelines, Armteqi may take appropriate action, including:

Action Description Severity
Friendly reminder A private note to clarify expectations Low
Warning A formal notice about the behavior and consequences Medium
Temporary restriction Limited access to community spaces for a set period Medium
Removal from the community Permanent removal from Armteqi spaces High
Permanent ban For severe or repeated violations High

Decisions are made fairly and with the goal of protecting the community. For more details, refer to our Code of Conduct.

14 Build Together

Learning technology should be exciting, not stressful. Armteqi is a place where you can build projects, make friends, and discover what you love.

  • Celebrate wins – share your achievements, big and small.
  • Collaborate – work together on projects and learn from each other.
  • Enjoy learning – celebrate the journey and the progress you make.
  • Leave it better than you found it – contribute positively and help the community grow.

Learn Together. Build Together. Grow Together. That's what Armteqi is about.
