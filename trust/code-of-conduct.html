Code of Conduct

Building a community we're proud of

Our commitment to creating a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who learns, builds, and grows with Armteqi.

Version 1.0 Effective: July 23, 2026 For all members

Everyone starts somewhere. Armteqi welcomes learners of all experience levels – from absolute beginners to seasoned practitioners. Questions are encouraged, mistakes are part of learning, and respectful guidance is valued over criticism. We celebrate collaboration over unhealthy competition.

01 Purpose

Armteqi was founded on the belief that technology education should be accessible to everyone. This Code of Conduct exists to protect that vision by creating a safe, respectful, and inclusive learning environment where every member can thrive.

We believe that the strongest communities are built on trust, honesty, and mutual respect. This document isn't just a set of rules – it's a reflection of our values and our commitment to each other.

Participation in Armteqi is a privilege, not a right. Community membership depends on following this Code of Conduct and contributing positively to our shared spaces.

02 Who It Applies To

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone in the Armteqi community, regardless of role, experience level, or how long you've been with us.

  • Members – anyone who participates in our community
  • Volunteers – those who give their time to help others
  • Mentors – experienced members who guide and support learners
  • Organizers – those who plan and manage community activities
  • Contributors – anyone who contributes to our open-source projects
  • Visitors – anyone who visits any Armteqi online or offline community spaces

By participating in Armteqi, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct in all community spaces, including online platforms, in-person events, and any communication related to our community.

03 Our Community Values

These are the principles that guide everything we do at Armteqi. They shape our decisions, our interactions, and our culture.

Respect everyone
Learn continuously
Help others grow
Act ethically
Be honest and transparent
Use technology responsibly
Protect privacy

04 Expected Behavior

We expect all community members to contribute to a positive, productive, and safe environment. Here's what that looks like in practice:

We encourage

  • Treating everyone with respect and kindness
  • Welcoming beginners and answering questions patiently
  • Giving constructive, actionable feedback
  • Sharing knowledge openly and generously
  • Respecting privacy and intellectual property
  • Following platform rules and applicable laws
  • Admitting when you don't know something
  • Learning from mistakes and helping others learn from theirs

We don't tolerate

  • Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
  • Hate speech or discrimination
  • Threats or personal attacks
  • Spam, scams, or deceptive behavior
  • Sharing malware, exploits, or malicious tools
  • Unauthorized hacking or illegal activities
  • Plagiarism or misrepresenting others' work
  • Deliberately derailing discussions or trolling

05 Responsible Security Learning

Armteqi focuses on cybersecurity education. This is a powerful responsibility that requires strong ethical standards.

We encourage ethical cybersecurity education. Members must never use community resources to perform unauthorized access, attacks, or illegal activities. Skills learned through Armteqi should only be used for defensive, educational, or authorized purposes.

Members should never request or provide assistance intended to compromise systems without authorization. If you're unsure whether an activity is ethical or legal, please ask before acting.

We'd rather have a conversation than have to take corrective action. When in doubt, reach out to the Armteqi Trust & Safety Team.

06 Academic Integrity

As a learning community, Armteqi takes academic integrity seriously. We believe that honest learning builds real skills and lasting confidence.

  • Don't cheat during CTFs, competitions, or learning activities
  • Don't plagiarize – always credit others for their work
  • Give proper attribution when using others' code, ideas, or content
  • Respect licenses – understand and follow open-source licensing terms
  • Share honestly – if you didn't create it, don't claim you did

Learning from others is encouraged. Passing off others' work as your own is not. We're here to grow together, not to take shortcuts.

07 Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the Armteqi Trust & Safety Team. We take all reports seriously and will respond as quickly as possible.

How to report: Send an email to hello@armteqi.org with as much detail as you're comfortable sharing. All reports will be handled with care and shared only with those who need to know in order to investigate and resolve the issue.

No retaliation. Armteqi does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports concerns in good faith. We are committed to protecting those who speak up.

08 Enforcement

When a violation of this Code of Conduct is reported or observed, the Armteqi Trust & Safety Team will take appropriate action. We aim to be fair, transparent, and consistent in our responses.

Before taking action, we may review available information, speak with those involved, and consider the severity, context, and impact of the behavior. Our goal is always to educate and rehabilitate when possible, and to protect the community when necessary.

Possible actions include:

Action Description Severity
Friendly reminder A private note to clarify expectations Low
Warning A formal notice about the behavior and consequences Medium
Temporary restriction Limited access to community spaces for a set period Medium
Removal from the community Permanent removal from Armteqi spaces High
Permanent ban For serious or repeated violations High

The severity of the response will depend on the nature of the violation, the impact on the community, and whether it's a first-time or repeated offense.

09 Appeals Process

We believe in fairness and second chances. Members who believe an enforcement decision was made in error may request a review by contacting the Armteqi Trust & Safety Team at armteqi.community@gmail.com.

In your appeal, please explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. The Trust & Safety Team will review the case and respond with a final decision. We treat all appeals with respect and due consideration.

10 Changes to This Code

As our community grows, this Code of Conduct may evolve. We will always publish the latest version in the Trust Center and notify members of significant changes through our communication channels.

We welcome feedback on this Code of Conduct. If you have suggestions for improvement, please reach out to us at armteqi.community@gmail.com.

11 Contact

If you have any questions about this Code of Conduct or need to report a concern, please reach out to the Armteqi Trust & Safety Team:

hello@armteqi.org Trust Center

We aim to respond to all inquiries as promptly as possible.
