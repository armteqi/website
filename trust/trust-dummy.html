Journal Entry #001

Building Armteqi

How Our Journey Began

August 2026 8 min read Behind the Scenes

“Every community has a story. This is how ours began.”

When people visit Armteqi today, they'll see a website, a logo, a GitHub organization, and a growing community. What they won't see is everything that came before. The uncertainty. The mistakes. The questions. The late nights. The moments when we wondered if we were making the right decisions.

Armteqi didn't begin with a website. It began with a question.

The Question

How many students are trying to build a future without the guidance they deserve?

Before we started building this community, I was just another student trying to figure out my own path. After completing my 10th standard, I had to make one of the first major decisions of my life. What should I study next?

Choosing a Path

Science never felt like my strongest subject, so Computer Science and Biology didn't seem like the right choices. That left me choosing between Humanities and Accountancy. I chose Accountancy.

At the time, I believed I understood what that decision meant. I didn't. I didn't know that different schools offered different subject combinations. Along with Accountancy, I found myself studying Computer Applications, Statistics, and Political Science—subjects I wasn't expecting and wasn't prepared for.

I worked hard, but something never felt right. It wasn't that I disliked learning. I simply wasn't sure I was learning the things that would help me build the future I wanted. That uncertainty stayed with me.

Discovery

Then, halfway through my higher secondary education, something changed. I discovered cybersecurity. Not because someone introduced me to it. Not because it was part of my syllabus. I found it the same way many students discover their passion today—through the internet.

One video became another. One article became ten. Soon, I was spending hours learning about ethical hacking, Linux, networking, digital security, and the people building careers in cybersecurity.

For the first time, learning didn't feel like an obligation. It became something I genuinely enjoyed.

The Reality Check

As I continued exploring the field, I kept hearing the same advice:

“Skills matter more than degrees.”

There is truth in those words. Skills do matter. But I misunderstood the message. I believed technical skills alone would be enough. Life eventually taught me that reality is more complex.

I didn't pass all of my Plus Two subjects on my first attempt. Like many students, I wondered if I had already fallen behind.

Instead of giving up, I completed my higher secondary education through NIOS and enrolled in a one-year Diploma in Cybersecurity. I gave it everything I had. I completed the diploma. I earned my Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) certification.

For a while, I thought I had reached the starting line of my career. Then I started applying for jobs.

That's when I learned one of the most important lessons of my journey. The internet had shown me one side of the story. The job market showed me the other.

Building a New Path

Skills were essential. But many opportunities also expected formal education. That realization forced me to stop making assumptions and start doing research.

I spent weeks reading job descriptions, comparing hiring requirements, understanding career paths, and asking one simple question: What are employers actually looking for?

The answer became clear. I decided to pursue a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) with a specialization in Cybersecurity. Even that journey wasn't straightforward. Several colleges rejected my application because I had completed my higher secondary education through NIOS. Eventually, one university gave me an opportunity.

Looking back today, I don't see those years as failures. I see them as lessons that changed the way I think. Every rejection taught me something. Every setback forced me to adapt. Every mistake made me ask better questions.

The Idea

Over time, one thought refused to leave my mind. What if another student is standing where I once stood?

What if someone in 8th, 9th, or 10th standard is trying to choose a future without understanding the options available? What if a Plus One or Plus Two student believes one wrong decision has already defined the rest of their life? What if someone simply needs a place to ask questions without being judged?

That thought eventually became something much bigger than my own career.

For years, I had imagined creating a community. Not a company. Not a coaching institute. A place where students could learn together, build projects together, ask questions freely, and grow together.

But like many ideas, I kept postponing it. "After my degree." "When I have more experience." "When the timing is right."

The Beginning

Then one day, something unexpected happened. A friend from my diploma course called me. During our conversation, he shared an idea that sounded remarkably similar to the one I had been carrying for years.

That conversation became the beginning. Soon, two more friends joined the journey. Instead of waiting for the perfect moment... We decided to begin.

That decision became Armteqi.

Our mission isn't simply to teach cybersecurity. It's to help students make informed decisions, create opportunities to learn, and build a community where curiosity is encouraged.

There came a point where I stopped counting the hours. We weren't trying to build a website. We weren't trying to create a logo. We were trying to build something we wished had existed when we were students.

  • If one student discovers a career because of Armteqi...
  • If one student decides not to give up after a setback...
  • If one student finds the confidence to ask their first question...

Then every challenge that brought us here will have been worth it.

This isn't a story about having everything figured out. It's a story about choosing to begin before everything was perfect.

“Because every community has a first day. This was ours.”

Welcome to Armteqi. This is where our journey begins.

— The Armteqi Team
