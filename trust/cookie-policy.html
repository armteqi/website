01 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit them. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language, theme settings, and other preferences.

Cookies are not viruses or malware. They are safe, widely used, and essential for many modern web experiences. At Armteqi, we use cookies responsibly and only when they genuinely improve your experience or help us maintain security.

02 How we use Cookies

At Armteqi, we take a privacy-first approach to cookies. We use them only for purposes that directly benefit you as a member of our community:

Security

Protecting your account and our platform from unauthorized access.

Preferences

Remembering your theme, language, and other display choices.

Analytics

Understanding how our community interacts with the website.

Marketing

We do not use marketing or advertising cookies.

We do not sell or share your data. Armteqi is a student-led, non-profit community. We do not run ads, and we never will. Your privacy is our priority.

03 Cookie categories

We classify cookies into the following categories. You can manage your preferences at any time using the Manage Cookie Preferences link in our footer.

Category Purpose Status
Essential Required for basic functionality, security, and core features. These cookies cannot be disabled. Always Enabled
Preferences Remember your settings, such as theme preference (light/dark mode) and language choices. Optional
Analytics Help us understand how you use Armteqi, anonymously. We use this to improve the website experience. Optional
Marketing Armteqi does not use marketing or advertising cookies. We never will. Not Used

When you visit Armteqi for the first time, only essential cookies are active. Optional cookies are only enabled after you give consent.

04 Manage your preferences

You have full control over your cookie preferences. You can change your choices at any time by clicking the Manage Cookie Preferences link in the footer of any page.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, but please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

05 Third-party cookies

Armteqi uses minimal third-party services. We carefully select partners who share our commitment to privacy:

  • GitHub – for hosting our open-source projects. GitHub's privacy policy applies to interactions with our repositories.
  • Font Awesome – for icons. We load icons via CDN, which may set minimal cookies for performance.
  • Google Fonts – for typography. We use the privacy-friendly embedding method.

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook) pixels, or any advertising trackers.

06 Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or regulatory reasons. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through our website or community channels.
