What we do

Three areas, one goal: making technology skills real, practical, and accessible.

Learn

Structured workshops and study groups covering fundamentals through advanced topics — taught by students, for students.

Build

Open-source projects and collaborative builds that turn theory into a working portfolio you can point to.

Secure

Hands-on cybersecurity practice — CTFs, labs, and threat-modeling sessions grounded in real-world security.

Why Armteqi

Principles we hold ourselves to, not just words on a page.

Free and open

No paywalls on core learning. Our materials and projects are open for anyone to use and contribute to.

Community-led

Run by students who understand what it's like to start from zero — every program is built with beginners in mind.

Security-first

Cybersecurity isn't a side track — it's woven into how we teach technology from the start.

Built to last

We optimize for clarity and trust over hype — practical skills you can rely on, not trends.
