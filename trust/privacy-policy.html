1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: directly from you (when you choose to provide it) and automatically through our website’s hosting infrastructure.

DataHow we obtain itPurpose
NameVoluntary (e.g., WhatsApp, emails, forms)Community interaction, support
Email addressWhen you contact usReplies, updates (if any)
Browser / device infoAutomatically processed by hosting providerSecurity, compatibility, analytics
Pages visitedAutomatically processed by hosting providerUnderstanding site usage, improvements

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific purpose and permitted by applicable law. We encourage participants not to submit sensitive personal information unless we specifically request it.

2. How We Use Information

  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Improve our website content and user experience
  • Organise community activities and share updates
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our services

We do not use your information for automated decision‑making or profiling.

3. No Sale of Personal Data

Armteqi does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. As a student‑led, non‑profit community, we have no advertisers, sponsors, or commercial interest in your data. Your privacy is not a product.

4. Cookies

Essential for site functionality (e.g., dark mode preference)
Advertising or tracking cookies
Third‑party analytics cookies (unless clearly disclosed)

We currently use only essential cookies or similar technologies required for the website to function properly, such as remembering your appearance preferences. If we introduce additional cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and explain how you can manage your preferences.

5. Third‑Party Services

  • WhatsApp – Our community group operates on WhatsApp. Your interactions are governed by WhatsApp’s privacy policy. If you join our WhatsApp community, administrators may be able to view the phone number, name, and profile information that you choose to make available through WhatsApp. We do not intentionally maintain a separate database of WhatsApp member information unless it is necessary for community administration or you voluntarily provide information through our forms or communication channels.
  • GitHub – We may use GitHub to host or manage parts of our website or open-source projects. GitHub may process certain technical information according to its own Privacy Statement. Armteqi does not intentionally disclose personal information to GitHub except where reasonably necessary for hosting our website or providing related services.
  • Social Media – If you engage with us on platforms like YouTube or Instagram, those platforms’ privacy rules apply. We do not receive private account details from them.

We carefully choose partners and do not share your information with them for their own marketing.

6. Data Security

While no system can guarantee absolute security, we implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of our services and the information we process.

We will take appropriate steps to notify affected individuals where required by applicable law and where practical.

7. Data Retention

  • Emails and contact submissions – We retain emails and contact submissions only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain our records, or comply with legal obligations.
  • Technical access logs – Retained for a limited time by our hosting provider (e.g., GitHub, Cloudflare) according to their standard policies. We do not independently store raw logs.
  • WhatsApp messages – We do not store or archive them outside WhatsApp.

You may request deletion of your information at any time. You may also withdraw any consent you have previously provided, where applicable (see "Your Rights").

8. Children’s Privacy

Armteqi provides educational programs for students, including secondary and higher secondary students. Some participants may be below the age of majority in their country.

Where required by applicable law, we may request verifiable parental or guardian consent before collecting personal information from children participating in our programs.

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our educational activities and do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information inappropriately, they may contact us and we will review and delete the information where appropriate.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights such as:

  • Access – ask what personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification – correct incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Erasure – request deletion of your personal data.
  • Objection – object to processing of your data.
  • Portability – receive your data in a machine‑readable format.

The rights available to you depend on the laws of your country or region. To exercise any of these rights, please email us at armteqi.community@gmail.com. We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will always show the most recent version. If we make material changes, we will post a notice on our website or community channels. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Responsible Disclosure

We appreciate the community’s help in keeping Armteqi safe. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly to armteqi.community@gmail.com. We will investigate and make every effort to address the issue quickly. We support good-faith security research conducted responsibly. Activities that intentionally disrupt our services, access unauthorized information, or violate applicable laws are not considered good-faith research.
