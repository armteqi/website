Why We Exist

Every student deserves the opportunity to build a future in technology. Many face financial barriers, limited guidance, or uncertainty about where to start. Armteqi bridges that gap.

We believe education should inspire confidence, not create barriers.

Vision

To create a future where every student, regardless of financial background, has access to quality technology education and a clear path into the industry.

Mission

Armteqi is a student-led community committed to free awareness programs, beginner bootcamps, peer learning, open source projects, and honest career guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

Is Armteqi free?
Yes, all current activities are completely free. Any future paid programs would be introduced transparently and only to sustain the community.
Who can join?
Anyone interested in learning technology – no prerequisites, no restrictions, no prior experience needed.
Do I need experience?
Not at all. Beginners are at the heart of our community. We built this exactly for you.
Is Armteqi only about cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity is a core focus, but we embrace all technology disciplines – from software development and cloud computing to AI and beyond.
Can I become a mentor?
We welcome active members to support their peers. Formal mentorship programs will be introduced once we have the capacity to do it well.
