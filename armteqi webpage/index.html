Why we exist

Every student deserves a future in technology. Many face financial barriers, limited guidance, or simply don't know where to start — Armteqi bridges that gap.

Vision

A future where every student, regardless of financial background, has access to quality technology education and a clear path into the industry.

Mission

Free awareness programs, beginner bootcamps, peer learning, open-source projects, and honest career guidance — run by students, for students.

What we do today

Real skills, real projects, and a supportive community to help you grow.

Cyber awareness

Free workshops helping students stay safe online and understand digital threats in a practical way.

Beginner bootcamps

Hands-on learning designed for students taking their very first steps into technology.

Open-source projects

Collaborative environments where members learn by building and contributing to real tools.

Study groups & peer learning

Structured group sessions and discussion circles that help you learn faster, together.

Career roadmaps

Clear, actionable learning paths and practical advice from industry professionals.
